## Content rules (apply to every future post)
- **No specific exercise/stretch instructions with technique detail.** Reps, holds, and form
  carry injury risk if described wrong in text — stick to general, low-risk guidance
  (movement, warmth, posture awareness, rest) rather than a stretch routine.
- **No medical claims.** Never "treats," "cures," "relieves arthritis," etc. — same
  comfort/support framing as product copy. Every post ends with a GP-referral disclaimer.
- **One collection link per post**, matched to the topic, placed naturally at the end.
- **Tag every post** with the matching `condition-*` tag plus `advice`, so posts can later
  be surfaced on relevant collection pages if desired.
