Returns and refunds

If your Jointwell wrap isn't right for you, you have 30 days from the day it is delivered to return it for a full refund of what you paid.

Changed your mind

Email support@velagoods.co.uk before sending anything back and we'll reply with the return address and simple instructions. Please send the wrap back with its cable and accessories.

We pay the return postage on change-of-mind returns. We'll send you a prepaid label, or reimburse the cost of a tracked service, and we refund the full price you paid once the wrap reaches us. Please keep your proof of postage until then.

If it arrives damaged, faulty, or isn't what you ordered

That is entirely on us. Email a photo and your order number, and choose whichever you prefer:

  • a full refund including all postage, or
  • a free replacement, shipped at our cost.

You will never be asked to pay return postage on a faulty item.

Two-year warranty

Every Jointwell wrap carries a two-year warranty from the date of delivery, covering faults in the product or its workmanship in normal household use. If it fails within that period, email us and we will replace it. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, deliberate damage, or wear from use outside the instructions supplied in the box. It is offered in addition to your statutory rights and does not replace them.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–10 working days of us receiving the return, or of us agreeing a refund on a faulty item. We'll email you when it's issued. If you paid with Klarna or another instalment provider, the refund is passed to them and they adjust your plan.

Cancelling before dispatch

If you change your mind before your order ships, email us and we'll cancel and refund it in full. Once an order has gone to our fulfilment partner we may be unable to stop it, in which case the 30-day return process above applies instead.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights. Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 you have the right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it, and a further 14 days to return the goods. Our 30-day promise is longer than that minimum and sits alongside it. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality; where they are not, you are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund.
