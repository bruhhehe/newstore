Last updated: 7 August 2026. This policy explains your rights under UK consumer law when you buy from Steadwell, plus any additional guarantees we offer.

Your legal right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you have the right to cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of receiving your goods, without needing to give a reason. To cancel, contact us via our Contact page before this period ends.

Once you've told us you want to cancel, you then have a further 14 days to send the goods back to us.

Our extended returns window

We want you to be genuinely happy with what you buy. In addition to your legal right above, Steadwell offers 30 days from the date of delivery to return an item you've changed your mind about, for a full refund.

Condition of returned goods

Items should be returned unused, in their original packaging where possible, with all accessories and documentation included. You are responsible for the item until it reaches us, so we recommend using a tracked delivery service.

Who pays for return postage

For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return postage costs. This does not apply to faulty or damaged items — see below.

Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible with photos where relevant. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, you're entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund, and we won't ask you to cover return postage in this case.

Refunds

Once we've received your returned item (or evidence that you've sent it back), we'll process your refund within 14 days, to your original payment method. We'll email you to confirm once it's on its way.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you'd like to start a return, please get in touch via our Contact page.
