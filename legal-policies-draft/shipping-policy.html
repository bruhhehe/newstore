Shipping

We ship to United Kingdom addresses only, and delivery is free and tracked on every order. There is no minimum spend and no delivery charge added at checkout.

How long it takes

We'd rather give you the honest range than a single number we can't keep.

  • Dispatch: around 9 in 10 orders leave us within 1–3 working days. A small number take longer at busy periods.
  • Delivery once dispatched: typically 4–8 days, with nearly all orders arriving within 12.
  • Most people have their order within a fortnight of placing it.

Every order is tracked, and we email the tracking link the moment it ships.

Priority handling

Priority handling is an optional paid add-on at checkout. It moves your order to the front of our dispatch queue. It does not change how long the courier then takes. If your order is not dispatched within 3 working days, we refund the priority handling fee.

If your tracking hasn't moved

This is normal mid-journey; parcels often scan on leaving, then not again until they reach the UK. If nothing has changed for more than seven days, email support@velagoods.co.uk and we'll chase it from our end.

If your order doesn't arrive

Get in touch and we'll sort it. A lost parcel is ours to resolve, not yours to chase. You'll get a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Addresses

Please check your delivery address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away; we can usually correct it before dispatch, but not after.
