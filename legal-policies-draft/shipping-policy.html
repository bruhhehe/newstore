Where we deliver

We currently deliver to addresses within the United Kingdom only.

Dispatch times

Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 business days of being placed. During busy periods this may take slightly longer — we'll let you know if there's a delay.

Delivery times & costs

  • Standard delivery: 3–5 working days — £4.99, free on orders over £40
  • Express delivery: 1–2 working days — £6.99
  • EU delivery: 7–14 working days — £14.99
  • International delivery (US, Canada, Australia and selected countries): 7–14 working days — £23.99

[Express delivery is not currently offered — this section can be updated if that changes.]

Tracking your order

Once your order has been dispatched, you'll receive a tracking email so you can follow its progress right up to your door.

If something goes wrong

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected window, or tracking shows something's not right, please get in touch via our Contact page and we'll look into it for you.

Questions
