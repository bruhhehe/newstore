This policy explains how Steadwell ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit or buy from our website, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect

  • Order & account information: name, delivery and billing address, email, phone number, and order history, collected when you place an order or create an account.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store your full card details ourselves.
  • Browsing information: pages visited, device and browser type, and similar analytics data, collected automatically via cookies.
  • Communications: messages you send us via our Contact page or live chat, and any product reviews you choose to submit.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and communicate with you about them
  • To respond to questions via our Contact page or live chat
  • To send you order-tracking updates
  • To send marketing emails, but only if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • To improve our website and product range
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this store, who process your data on our behalf:

  • Shopify — our e-commerce platform, which hosts our store and processes orders and payments
  • Judge.me — our reviews provider, which may email you after an order to request a review
  • Track123 — our delivery tracking provider, which sends shipment tracking updates
  • Shopify Inbox — our live chat provider, used if you message us via chat

We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and understand how our site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; turning some off may affect how the site works.

How long we keep your information

We keep order information for 6 years from the date of the transaction, in line with standard UK tax and accounting record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, in certain circumstances
  • Object to or restrict how we use your information
  • Ask for your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Contact page. If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your request, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
